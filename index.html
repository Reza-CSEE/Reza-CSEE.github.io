Jun 2025 – Present

Independent research: detecting AI-generated voices

Rastin-Voice

Voice cloning now takes seconds, and most voice authentication only checks that a voice matches a stored one, not that it is human. I built a detector that works from the signal itself, so it can flag voices from systems it was never tuned on.

  • Designed a signal-level detector that tells human speech from AI-generated and cloned voices, without training a neural network on known voice generators.
  • Validated on 7 datasets spanning 5 voice-synthesis architecture families, reaching AUC 0.94–0.99 on realistic test sets, including In-the-Wild and ASVspoof 5, on noisy real-world audio.
  • Audited the pipeline for data leakage and fixed several causes of falsely perfect results: shared data pools, silent file-loading failures and sign errors.
  • Built and deployed an interactive demo with Python, Gradio and Hugging Face Spaces, with uncompressed 32-bit browser recording, a live spectrogram and a 10-second live verdict.
